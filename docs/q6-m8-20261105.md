# x64 Q6 M8 optimization

The Q6 tiled narrow path computes eight valid activation positions instead of executing its padded sixteen-position microtile. On AVX-VNNI-INT8, it also uses signed byte dot products and skips the activation offset and weight correction. Other shapes and quantization formats retain their existing paths. AVX2 without signed-byte VNNI retains the eight-position specialization with the original arithmetic; AVX512 VNNI retains its existing path.

The selected change modifies only `ggml/src/ggml-cpu/tiled/tiled.cpp`, `tiled-kernel.cpp`, and `tiled-kernel.h`. It preserves model quantization, KV format and sampling. The older kernel already acquired some word-dot VNNI instructions through compiler optimization; this change adds a specific signed-byte path.

On the Core Ultra 7 255H, the initial three process orders give:

| History | Historical Q8-signed build M8 (ms) | Q6 candidate M8 (ms) | Cost reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 128 | 123.484 | 108.845 | 11.9% |
| 512 | 137.955 | 121.431 | 12.0% |

These are cross-session pure forward results. The historical executable was not rerun. The fourth, predeclared observation is retained separately; the selected candidate's D512 process means are 121.211, 121.065, 122.018 and 122.942 ms. Its four-process M8/M1 ratios are 2.354 and 2.588.

All five experimental candidates completed 20 model processes and 1120 formal observations with full-logit hashes matching the historical reference. The selected Q6 fixture passed 1296 scalar, full-width bitwise, stride, tail and INT8-extreme checks. Related translation units compile for AVX2 and AVX512 fallback configurations. Indexed matmul received a caller review but was not exercised by this Spark model.

A Q6/Q4/preparation combination has a lower average M8 cost, but its D512 observations depend strongly on execution order. It remains experimental, along with the Q4-only and preparation-only candidates. Selection of the Q6-only change favors scope and the observed D512 consistency; it does not establish that it is the fastest mean configuration.
